I am a postdoctoral researcher in the [Resource-Aware Data Systems (RAD) Group](https://rad.itu.dk/) at the IT University of Copenhagen. I am a computer systems researcher working at the intersection of efficient AI infrastructure, GPU resource management, and data-intensive computing.

My research develops measurement-driven methods, predictive models, and runtime systems that make modern computing platforms more efficient, predictable, and accessible. I am particularly interested in resource-aware scheduling, GPU workload characterization, and reproducible systems research for deep learning and large-scale data processing.
